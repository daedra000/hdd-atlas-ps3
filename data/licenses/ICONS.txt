HDD Atlas's icons

The hard disk (the XMB's icon, ICON0.PNG, and the one beside "HDD Atlas" at
the top of the app's screens) is "Internal Clear" by Rick Patrick, from
SoftIcons (https://www.softicons.com). It's trimmed and resized for both.


The START menu's icons

The START menu's and the start screen's icons are by Tanah Basah, from
Flaticon (https://www.flaticon.com), used under Flaticon's free licence,
which asks for this credit. They were made white and resized; the rectangle
is cut into boxes for "Map of the drive", and Identical files' pages have an "="
cut into them.
