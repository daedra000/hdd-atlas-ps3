The verdict	结论
Good: no worn sectors, nothing past its threshold, and not running hot. Caution: the drive works, but some sectors have worn out, an old-age attribute is past its threshold, or it's above 55 °C: keep a backup of what matters. Failing: a pre-fail attribute is at or below its threshold, so the drive itself expects to fail soon: copy off what you want to keep.	良好：没有磨损的扇区，没有属性到达阈值，也没有过热。注意：硬盘仍能工作，但有些扇区已磨损、某个老化属性已越过阈值，或温度高于 55 °C：请备份重要内容。即将故障：某个预故障属性已到达或低于阈值，说明硬盘自己预计很快会坏：请把想保留的内容复制出来。

Bad sectors	坏扇区
Reallocated: sectors that went bad and were swapped for spares. Pending: sectors the drive couldn't read, waiting to be rewritten or swapped. Uncorrectable: reads that failed even after error correction, so what was there may be lost. On a healthy drive all three are 0. A few isn't fatal, but a count that keeps growing is the surest sign a drive is on its way out.	重新分配：已损坏并被备用扇区替换的扇区。待处理：硬盘读不出、正等待重写或替换的扇区。无法纠正：即使经过纠错仍然失败的读取，其中的内容可能已经丢失。健康的硬盘三项都是 0。少量不算致命，但一直在增长的数字是硬盘快要报废的最可靠信号。
The table	表格
The drive scores each attribute. Value is the score now (usually 100 or 200 when new; lower is worse), Worst is the lowest it's ever been, and Threshold is the maker's limit: at or below it, that attribute has failed. Raw is the drive's own count behind the score (hours, sectors, degrees). What it means differs from maker to maker, so compare a drive with its own earlier readings, not with another drive.	硬盘会给每个属性打分。当前值是现在的分数（新盘通常为 100 或 200，越低越差），最差值是历来最低的分数，阈值是厂商定的界限：到达或低于它，该属性即已失效。原始值是分数背后硬盘自己的计数（小时、扇区、度数）。其含义因厂商而异，所以请把硬盘和它自己以前的读数比较，而不是和另一块硬盘比较。
Pre-fail and old-age	预故障与老化
The drive marks every attribute as one or the other. Pre-fail attributes (marked pre-fail in the table) watch for the faults that come before a breakdown: bad sectors, slow spin-up, seek errors. One at or below its threshold means the drive expects to fail soon. The rest are old-age attributes: they follow ordinary wear over the years, like hours and power cycles. One past its threshold means the drive is worn, not that it's about to stop.	硬盘会把每个属性标为两类之一。预故障属性（表格中标有“预故障”）监视故障之前出现的问题：坏扇区、启动转速慢、寻道错误。其中任何一项到达或低于阈值，就表示硬盘预计很快会坏。其余的是老化属性：它们记录多年来的正常磨损，例如通电时间和通电次数。老化属性越过阈值，说明硬盘已经老旧，但不代表它马上会停止工作。
SSDs	SSD
An SSD shows its life left in place of power cycles: its own estimate of how much of its flash's rated wear is left, 100% when new. At 10% or under the verdict says Caution. On an SSD, reallocated counts worn-out blocks of flash swapped for spares, and the verdict waits for 70 °C, as SSDs run warmer. A failing SSD can stop answering instead of reporting errors: if a scan freezes at the same place every time, suspect the drive even when SMART looks good.	SSD 会显示剩余寿命，代替通电次数：这是它自己估算的闪存额定磨损还剩多少，新盘为 100%。降到 10% 或以下时，结论为“注意”。在 SSD 上，重新分配的扇区是被备用块替换掉的磨损闪存块；由于 SSD 运行温度更高，结论要到 70 °C 才算过热。故障中的 SSD 可能直接停止响应，而不是报告错误：如果扫描每次都卡在同一个位置，即使 SMART 看起来正常，也要怀疑驱动器。
History	历史
Every reading is kept: Square shows them, newest first. A reallocated, pending or uncorrectable count that grew since the reading before shows in yellow. 3. Don't scan again and again	3. 不要反复扫描
A scan reads every folder on the drive. Where the drive can't read, a scan can stop (HDD Atlas gives up after 5 minutes), and a drive that keeps retrying wears faster.	扫描会读取硬盘上的每个文件夹。在硬盘读不了的地方，扫描可能会停住（HDD Atlas 会在 5 分钟后放弃），而不断重试的硬盘磨损更快。
4. Replace the drive	4. 更换硬盘
A worn drive can't be mended, and it only gets worse. The PS3's Backup Utility is only a second safety net: it keeps no trophies, takes hours, and can fail on a damaged file. Copying the drive on a PC doesn't work: it's encrypted to this console.
